About Denise

A guide, not just a practitioner.

Twenty years of holistic practice, built on her own journey back to health.

My story

Wellness found me before I found it.

My own path into holistic health started in the late 1980s with a Gilbert Syndrome diagnosis, followed by my children's food sensitivities. Conventional answers fell short, so I went looking for better ones. That search for non-traditional solutions became a quest for knowledge and understanding, combined with my passion to teach and share my knowledge, culminated into fulfilling my purpose, passion and leading to establishing a career.

Denise is an overall wellness strategist drawing from over 20 years of professional experience while incorporating personal experience and triumphs over Lyme Disease, Breast Implant Illness, and CIRS (mold illness). She is a Quantum Living Advocate offering Bioresonance Therapy and Frequency Scanning. As a Certified Thermography Specialist, she strives to raise awareness regarding early detection for both men and women. She holds a Masters with High Honors in Holistic Nutrition and a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology. As a Colon Hydrotherapist she is certified by the International Association for Colon Hydrotherapy. She is a LEAP trained therapist through Oxford Biomedical Technologies. She also authored the wellness based cookbook, To Our Health.

My philosophy

The soil beneath the tree.

My philosophy of wellness reflects the simplicity and magnificence found in nature. I see the human experience much like a tree: what becomes visible above the surface is deeply influenced by the environment that nourishes it below.

In our lives, the soil represents everything that impacts our wellness and quality of living. The physical, emotional and spiritual dimensions of our lives is our soil. When this inner environment is supported and replenished, it provides a stronger foundation from which the whole person can thrive.

This is why my approach looks beyond what is most visible. Rather than tending only to the leaves, the branches, or even the roots, I seek to understand and nourish the soil beneath them. By supporting the physical, emotional, and spiritual foundations of wellness, we cultivate the conditions for greater balance, resilience, and lasting growth.

True wellness is not found by tending to one part of the tree. It begins by nourishing the soil from which the whole person grows and thrives.
