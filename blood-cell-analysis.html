Blood Cell Analysis

A single drop of blood reveals the root cause of imbalance and disease, in real time.

The partnership

Denise Souza Wellness & Dr. Hilu of Hilu Clinic, Spain.

Denise has partnered with Dr. Hilu of Hilu Clinic, Spain to offer blood cell analysis in the USA. Several drops of peripheral blood from the fingertip provide an in-depth analysis of the current state of the patient's blood cells through extreme microscope magnification, up to 65,000x.

Blood Cell Analysis reveals the biological makeup of each individual, with different cellular patterns commonly linked to specific health challenges. By seeing what is happening within the cells throughout the body, abnormalities can be addressed using an all-natural approach.

"Everything is related to everything." — Dr. Hilu. Address the root issue, not the individual symptoms, and the rest of the body follows.

Early detection. Root causes. Personalized protocol.

Food intolerances

How it works

From fingertip to personalized protocol.

A simple fingertip prick provides the sample. That single drop of blood is examined under high magnification and the analysis is viewed together in real time, revealing what's actually happening inside your body right now.

After the blood test is analyzed, Dr. Hilu provides a customized, personal protocol designed to optimize good health and prevent illness. This protocol suggests therapies, supplements, dietary changes, and lifestyle adjustments specific to your results.

Denise then meets with you for a 30-minute consultation to review findings and integrate Dr. Hilu's protocol into your broader wellness plan.
